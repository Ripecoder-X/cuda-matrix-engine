## Done
- Selected one hypothesis from the Batch 7.1 profile: optimized tile 32 was MIO-throttle limited at
  13.84 cycles with zero bank conflicts, so increasing the register micro-tile from 2x2 to 4x4 should
  halve shared-memory loads per FMA.
- Changed only `src/cuda/kernels/gemm_optimized.cu`: `kMicro = (TILE == 32) ? 4 : 2`, block
  `TILE / kMicro` (tile 32 becomes 8x8), 4x4 accumulators, per-step vector loads of four A and four
  B values, and a fully unrolled multiply and write. Tile 16 remains 2x2.
- Re-ran correctness, the kernel-only benchmark, and Nsight Compute after the change.
- Kept the change: kernel-only median GFLOP/s improved by 52.7% at 512, 87.8% at 1024, 88.7% at
  2048, and 78.2% at 4096, each above the 5% threshold and larger than the IQR overlap.
- Did not claim size 256: it was 21.4% slower (0.011264 ms baseline to 0.014336 ms).
- Recorded the before/after numbers and the Nsight Compute comparison, including report filenames,
  in `docs/PROFILING.md`; updated `docs/ALGORITHMS.md` and `docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md`.
- After-profile result: duration 2.73 ms to 1.42 ms, MIO throttle 13.84 to 0.05 cycles, achieved
  occupancy 96.30% to 39.57%, bank conflicts 0/0 to 70,173 loads / 2,065,317 stores.
